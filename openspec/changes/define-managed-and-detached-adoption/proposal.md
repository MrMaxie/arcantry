# Why

Removing Arcantry-managed metadata is not the same operation as transferring selected capabilities into permanently project-owned scripts, skills or guidance. Treating both outcomes as removal hides ongoing dependencies, licensing, compatibility, update, support and maintenance consequences and can produce an implementation that is still coupled to Arcantry despite different branding.

# What changes

- Define one-way detachment as an explicit reviewed ownership transfer distinct from managed removal.
- Require a plan covering selected capabilities, transformations, omissions, copied and reimplemented material, project-owned identities, licenses and the exact post-transfer owner.
- Bound detached outputs with a capability budget and a negative dependency contract.
- Verify independence from a fresh checkout without Arcantry runtime, build, CI, network, user-scope, private-path, branding or update dependencies.
- Treat later comparison, re-adoption or migration as a new explicit transition with conflict review.
- Explain what managed Arcantry continues to provide and what a detached project gives up.

# Out of scope

- Automatically synchronizing detached outputs with later Arcantry versions.
- Exporting an entire reusable Arcantry distribution into another repository.
- Removing license, copyright or attribution obligations.
- Defining two named ownership modes across every product surface before the separate exploration is accepted.
