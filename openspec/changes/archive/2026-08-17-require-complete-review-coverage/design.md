# Decisions

## Coverage follows the requested surface

The skill creates an explicit coverage list only when the requested scope is an entire repository or another complete surface. Narrow reviews continue to honor the selected diff, files or contracts without artificial expansion.

## Unreviewed areas remain visible

A material area may be explicitly unreviewed when evidence or access is unavailable, but the skill cannot convert that limitation into a complete-coverage claim.

## Review authorization remains unchanged

Coverage accounting is part of read-only inspection. It does not authorize edits, tests, publication or advancement of findings.
